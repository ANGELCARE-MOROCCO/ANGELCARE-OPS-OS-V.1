# Known Limitations at Delivery Time

- Live provider calls were not performed while packaging.
- The user's authenticated browser routes were not executed while packaging.
- Browser visual quality and accessibility require live evidence.
- Existing purpose-built workspace interfaces are preserved; IC10 does not replace them with one generic template.
- Existing business data may produce integrity findings that require human reconciliation.
- The optional Playwright runner requires Playwright to be available in the user's project.
- Certification status begins as `NOT TESTED`; this is intentional and cannot be bypassed by installation.

- Static design diversity does not certify browser rendering, workflow correctness or subjective visual excellence.
- Board sign-off remains impossible until all 15 governed workspace records and all eight required scenarios are live-certified.

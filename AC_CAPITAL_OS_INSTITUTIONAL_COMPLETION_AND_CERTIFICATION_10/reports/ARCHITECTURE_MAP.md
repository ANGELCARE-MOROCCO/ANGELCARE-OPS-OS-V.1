# IC10 Canonical Architecture

```text
Tavily/OpenRouter Research
  → Radar Source
  → Evidence Cluster
  → Canonical Opportunity
  → Funder Intelligence
  → Qualification Dossier
  → Proof Requirements / Data Room
  → Funding Case
  → Capital Pipeline
  → Universal Exact-Version Approval
  → Coordinator Mission
  → Atomic Manual Submission Proof
  → Outcome
  → Learning
```

The Capital Executive Orchestrator processes internal events. External communication and submission remain human-controlled. IC10 certification evidence is stored separately from business truth and never changes a business outcome merely to obtain a passing score.

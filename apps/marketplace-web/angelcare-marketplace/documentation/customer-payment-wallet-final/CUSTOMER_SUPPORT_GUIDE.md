# Customer Support Guide

Support can explain and investigate, but cannot fabricate a balance, confirmation or refund.

## Common cases

- **Top-up pending:** verify payment intent and provider event; do not manually credit unless Finance authorizes a separate goodwill adjustment.
- **Wallet reservation remains:** verify external payment state; release only through the reservation function and preserve evidence.
- **Saving not displayed:** inspect active policy dates, targets, stacking, margin guardrails and customer assignment.
- **Refund pending:** show Wallet restoration and external refund separately.
- **Account duplication:** use verified identity evidence; never merge balances blindly.
- **Frozen Wallet:** open the risk case, explain restrictions, preserve customer value and follow recovery procedure.

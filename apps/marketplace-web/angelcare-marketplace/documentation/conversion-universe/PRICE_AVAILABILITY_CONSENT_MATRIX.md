# Price, availability and consent matrix

Price snapshots expire after 30 minutes. Availability holds expire after 20 minutes. Basket and checkout revalidation inspect every basket line. Quote-only or unresolved lines never receive a fabricated total.

Mandatory confirmation consents: Marketplace terms and privacy notice. Health-adjacent journeys additionally require explicit non-medical-boundary acknowledgement. Journey-specific consents may be recorded for Academy, Partner OS, Quality and order/quote scope.

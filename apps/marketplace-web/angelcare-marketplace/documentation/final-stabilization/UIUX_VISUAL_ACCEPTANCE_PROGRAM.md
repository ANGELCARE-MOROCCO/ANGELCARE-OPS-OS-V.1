# ANGELCARE Marketplace — UI/UIX Visual Acceptance Programme

## Quality law

The accepted Homepage Flagship is the minimum quality floor. Remaining surfaces must preserve their purpose-built identities rather than collapse into one generic template.

## Viewport matrix

- Desktop: 1440 × 1000
- Tablet: 1024 × 1366
- Mobile: 390 × 844

## Locale matrix

- French canonical business language;
- complete English route and copy review;
- complete Arabic route and copy review;
- structural RTL for navigation, timeline, drawers, tables, icons, status progression and mobile action bars.

## State matrix

Every relevant page must be observed in:

- loading;
- empty;
- populated;
- error;
- unavailable;
- validation failure;
- blocked;
- permission denied;
- success;
- archived or completed.

## Automatic evidence

`capture-visual-evidence.mjs` captures full-page screenshots and records:

- HTTP status;
- route redirection;
- blank/error markers;
- horizontal overflow;
- broken images;
- missing media alternatives;
- unnamed links/buttons;
- unlabeled form controls;
- duplicate IDs;
- document language and direction;
- resource transfer and DOM size;
- page and console errors.

## Human acceptance

Automated success does not replace expert review of:

- visual hierarchy;
- emotional fit by audience;
- business language;
- readability;
- card density;
- mobile reachability;
- contrast;
- keyboard sequence;
- screen-reader output;
- real data realism;
- the absence of raw technical language.

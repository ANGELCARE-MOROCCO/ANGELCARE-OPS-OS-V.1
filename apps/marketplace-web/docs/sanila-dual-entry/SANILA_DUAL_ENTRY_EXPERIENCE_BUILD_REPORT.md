# SANILA Dual Entry Experience — Build Report

## Mission

`SANILA_DUAL_ENTRY_EXPERIENCE_PREMIUM_EVOLUTION`

The command-center entry is now principal-aware and server-authoritative. The exact governed SANILA Master Demo receives a commercial Experience Center; every normal customer context receives a separate Executive Command Center driven only by that school's data and the viewer's established permissions.

## Master Demo Experience Center

- Premium living-school hero bound to the existing Master Demo business clock.
- 14 real SANILA product universes with deep links to existing workspaces.
- Five guided journeys plus free exploration and resume behavior.
- Living-school evidence from the established operational dataset rather than duplicated marketing fixtures.
- Visit progression, visited modules, favorites, priorities, questions, recommended next module and personalized recap.
- Commercial intent capture for guided demo, proposal, contact and summary actions.
- Persistent visit state reuses the governed append-only `sanila_demo_access_events` authority.
- Deep-route tracking is enabled only after the server has classified the exact trusted Master Demo principal.
- No showroom action writes to the 127,177-row operational school dataset.

## Real Tenant Executive Command Center

- Separate production experience with no Demo language, progress, favorites or commercial journey controls.
- School-scoped operational metrics, seven-day attendance trend, six-month finance trend, admissions funnel and action center.
- Academic, payroll, transport, library, inventory, quality and calendar entry points.
- No fabricated zero when a permitted source fails: partial/unavailable sources are signaled explicitly.
- Sensitive real-tenant domains are permission/role gated before service-role-backed queries execute; restricted domains render as restricted rather than exposing values.
- Customer business time remains real time through the existing business-clock authority.

## Isolation and non-regression

- Trusted classifier requires exact `SANILA_MASTER_DEMO_SCHOOL_ID`, trusted `demoAccess`, and matching Demo school ID.
- No pathname, school-name, label or query-parameter heuristic grants Demo authority.
- Real tenant and delegated-customer contexts default to the production Executive Command Center.
- Existing AngelCare 360 shell, entitlements, support banner, permissions and 195 workspace routes remain the product kernel.
- Existing Master Demo non-billable/safety/side-effect doctrine is not modified.
- No new SQL, migration, seed or reset is required by this build.

## Demo Desk evolution

The existing Demo Desk now replays governed experience events per grant and surfaces exploration progress, favorites, priorities, prospect questions, last activity and conversion intent alongside existing PIN/grant controls.

## Validation boundary

This source capsule can perform static/syntax certification without dependency installation or a production build. Live browser acceptance must still verify the exact Master Demo, a normal tenant, and a delegated customer context after the normal controlled release pipeline. The permanent Master Demo seed must not be rerun for this UI mission.

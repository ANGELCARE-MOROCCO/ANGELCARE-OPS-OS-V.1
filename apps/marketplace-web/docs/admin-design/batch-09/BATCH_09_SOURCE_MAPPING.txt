15. CROSS-WORKSPACE BOUNDARIES CODEX MUST PRESERVE
======================================================================

  Trainer assignment may depend on provider eligibility.
  Certification events may affect provider eligibility.
  Provider master data remains Prestataires & fournisseurs authority.

  Academy B2B training orders retain organization / CRM / tenant lineage.
  Organization commercial relationship remains B2B/CRM authority.

B2B <-> PARTNER OS:
  Organization360 carries tenant_id relation.
  Diagnostic conversion can create a partner tenant through canonical conversion.
  Partner OS owns tenant lifecycle, subscription, modules and onboarding.

B2B <-> CRM:
  Organization360 carries crm_account_id.
  B2B Command links to existing commercial pipeline rather than replacing it.

B2B <-> OPERATIONS:
  Active programs can create downstream operational work, but Operations Batch 07
  remains the execution/reconciliation authority.

B2B <-> FINANCE:
  Monetary amounts may be displayed contextually, but invoices/payments/settlement
  authority remains Finance.

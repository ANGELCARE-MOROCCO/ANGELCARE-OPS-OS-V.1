## Ownership model

`sanila_demo_fixture_registry` records `(config_id, table_name, fixture_key, fixture_id, seed_version, domain_key)` for every deterministic row. It is service-role-only and RLS-enabled.

Fixture UUIDs remain stable through the existing `sanila_master_demo_fixture_uuid(config_id, fixture_key)` authority. The enhanced upsert filters payload fields through the current table definition, rejects missing required columns, performs `ON CONFLICT (id)` updates, and registers ownership only after a successful insert/update.

Unregistered rows are outside reset authority even when they belong to the demo school.

## Foreign-key rules that govern ordering

- Identity/configuration: `sanila_demo_configs.operator_tenant_id → operator_tenants.id`; `school_id → schools.id`; Admin/actors → `app_users.id`.
- Structure: terms/classes/sections/class-subjects/enrollments/timetable rows depend on school and academic year; sections depend on classes.
- People: student-parent links depend on students and parents; emergency contacts use a polymorphic student ID; staff contracts depend on staff.
- Academic: submissions depend on assignments and students; exam sessions/marks depend on exams; report-card lines depend on report cards, subjects, and comments.
- Attendance: records depend on sessions and students; justifications depend on records.
- Finance: fee items depend on fee structures; fee assignments depend on structures/students; invoice lines depend on invoices; payments depend on invoices/students; receipts depend on payments; reminders depend on invoices.
- Transport: routes depend on vehicles/staff; stops depend on routes; assignments depend on year/student/route/vehicle/stops.
- Library: copies depend on books; loans depend on copies and student/staff borrowers.
- Inventory: items depend on categories; movements depend on items.
- Communications: recipients depend on messages and parents; participants depend on conversations and a participant identity.
- Reports: templates/requests/exports depend on reports; requests depend on templates; export files depend on report exports.
- Reset history and access/security tables are never registered as fixtures and are never deleted by reset.

## Current-schema compatibility policy

- Required Living School tables fail closed if absent or if a new non-null/no-default column is not supplied.
- Payload properties absent from a deployed table are ignored by the schema-adaptive helper; required table columns are not ignored.
- Optional operational history/approval tables remain zero-state until a user performs the corresponding demo action. This is intentional; no fake approval, provider call, file, GPS event, or external delivery is asserted.
- The frozen early-September baseline remains the high-volume base graph. Living School V2 adopts its deterministic rows, replaces shallow collections, and aligns current-state fixtures to the sole 2027-02-17 business instant.

## Safety invariants

1. No `INSERT` targets schools, tenants, app users, roles, or role links.
2. All contacts use `.invalid` addresses and non-routable `+212000…` numbers.
3. National IDs are null; files are zero-byte placeholder records with nonexistent paths.
4. External reminders/exports are `blocked`, `processing_locked`, `cancelled`, or `blocked_not_configured`.
5. Reset deletes only registry-owned IDs inside one exception-safe transactional block.
6. Any foreign reference from an unregistered row causes reset to abort and roll back fixture deletion.

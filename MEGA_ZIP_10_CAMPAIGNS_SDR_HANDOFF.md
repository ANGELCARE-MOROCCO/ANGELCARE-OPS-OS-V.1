# Mega ZIP 10 Handoff — Campaigns, SDR, Sequences & Revenue Attribution

Phase 9 completes the direct-family commercial cycle. Mega ZIP 10 should consume:
- canonical prospects, accounts and opportunities;
- communication events;
- appointments and meeting outcomes;
- proposals, contracts, payment confirmations and realization;
- partnership referral attribution;
- B2C source and conversion events.

It must complete audience segmentation, recipient membership, multichannel sequence steps, provider delivery events, reply/opt-out governance, SDR queues, meeting/opportunity/proposal/contract/payment attribution, experiments, cost control and realized-return reporting without duplicating the communication or finance ledgers.

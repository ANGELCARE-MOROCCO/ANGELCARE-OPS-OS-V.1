# Mega ZIP 8 — Authenticated E2E Acceptance Procedure

Use noncritical test records and an authorized Revenue user.

1. Create and qualify a test partner.
2. Add sponsor, economic decision-maker and operational owner.
3. Open the canonical partnership opportunity.
4. Use Phase 6 and Phase 7 to create the accepted agreement and activation context.
5. Create a partner program, configured service line and at least one mandatory activation gate.
6. Evaluate and approve launch only after gates pass.
7. Register a referral with source evidence and contactability context.
8. Verify duplicate review for an existing prospect; otherwise accept and convert transactionally.
9. Link the resulting prospect/opportunity.
10. Record a verified event attribution and confirm total event share cannot exceed 100%.
11. Record a realized-revenue attribution only against a valid Phase 7 realization event.
12. Create a performance period, record metrics and close it atomically.
13. Verify scorecard, review and recommendation.
14. Approve renewal, then launch canonical Proposal or Negotiation and verify the linked IDs.
15. Create expansion or recovery as appropriate.
16. Verify all timelines, audit events, RLS and absence of duplicate referrals/attributions.
17. Reverse a test realization only in an isolated test scenario and verify the related attribution becomes reversed.

All steps must return real persisted records. No frontend-only success is accepted.

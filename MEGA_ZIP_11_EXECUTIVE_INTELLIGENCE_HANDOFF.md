# Mega ZIP 11 Handoff — Executive Intelligence, Forecasting and Orchestration

Mega ZIP 10 exposes trustworthy campaign inputs for the next phase:

- Frozen audience and eligible/enrolled volumes
- Channel/provider/sender readiness
- Distinct dispatch, delivery, reply and opt-out states
- SDR workload and outcomes
- Meeting/opportunity/proposal/contract/payment/realization lineage
- Evidence-backed campaign attribution
- Estimated versus confirmed campaign costs
- Immutable campaign performance periods
- Deliverability, compliance, conversion and recovery risks

Mega ZIP 11 must consume these structures without creating a second attribution or forecasting ledger. It should unify B2B, partnerships, B2C and campaigns into executive forecasting, leakage detection, scenario planning and intervention orchestration.

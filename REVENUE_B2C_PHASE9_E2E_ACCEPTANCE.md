# Revenue B2C Phase 9 — Authenticated E2E Acceptance

Use a noncritical test family and authorized test users.

1. Create the family dossier.
2. Add a primary guardian.
3. Add a beneficiary using only required data.
4. Add emergency contact and family instructions.
5. Record service requirements.
6. Complete needs assessment and qualification.
7. Schedule and record a consultation.
8. Create and approve a service recommendation.
9. Launch the Phase 6 quotation flow and record acceptance.
10. Create a matching cycle.
11. Add at least two candidates.
12. Verify availability for the selected candidate.
13. Present and accept the match.
14. Link the Phase 7 contract.
15. Confirm authoritative payment state.
16. Create and complete onboarding.
17. Create and accept operational handoff.
18. Evaluate activation gates.
19. Authorize and record care start.
20. Record satisfaction.
21. Create and close one complaint or retention risk.
22. Launch and complete a recovery checkpoint.
23. Confirm status history, evidence and audit.
24. Confirm no duplicate prospect, contract, payment or caregiver record was created.

## Pass criteria

- Every mutation succeeds only with permission.
- Unverified caregiver availability blocks match acceptance.
- Missing contract/payment/handoff gates block care start.
- Sensitive data is not exposed to unauthorized users.
- Audit and status history reflect the cycle.
